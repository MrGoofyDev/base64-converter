Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Image to Base64 handles information when you use this website.

Images and conversion

Image conversion is performed locally in your browser. The tool does not require or make an upload of your selected image or pasted Base64 data to our server. Your image data remains on your device while you use the converter.

The website itself is delivered by its hosting provider, which may process standard connection information as part of providing and securing the service. Refer to the hosting provider's privacy information for its practices.

Advertising and cookies

The homepage loads an advertising script from highrevenueformat.com. The script may connect your browser to advertising providers and their partners. Those providers may use cookies, pixels, local storage, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads, subject to your region, browser settings, and applicable consent requirements. These technologies are controlled by the advertising providers, not by the image converter.

We do not currently make a claim about which specific cookies an ad provider may set in a particular visit; this can vary by ad, location, and provider configuration. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any consent controls presented on the site.

Third-party privacy information

When advertising is enabled, your browser may connect to Adsterra and its advertising partners. Those parties may receive information such as your IP address, browser or device details, and interactions necessary to serve or measure advertising. Their processing is governed by their own policies. Review Adsterra's privacy policy and the policies of any other providers whose content is shown.

This website does not control third-party websites or services. Their policies and practices may change independently.
